# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Educators, AVS professionals, specialists, families, and institutional partners using French or Arabic to discover and complete professional inclusive-education learning.

## Product Purpose

ANEI provides bilingual professional learning, webinars, educational resources, verifiable certificates, and access to certified AVS profiles. Success means helping people move from knowledge to practical inclusive-education work.

## Positioning

ANEI combines professional inclusive-education learning and field-oriented resources in one bilingual French/Arabic platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing routes, authentication, course search, server-side authorization, database-backed content, and the modular monolith. Public, learner, and admin experiences remain visually distinct. Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials, or functionality.

## Brand Commitments

ANEI is professional, institutional, premium, human, trustworthy, inclusive, academic, and modern. French and Arabic receive equal visual quality with genuine RTL behavior.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository-backed course, webinar, resource, news, AVS, and homepage count data; local documentary learning photography in `public/media`. No testimonials or external institutional claims are approved.

## Product Principles

- Human and educational before ornamental.
- Product truth and working behavior before presentation.
- Clear learning discovery and continuation paths.
- Bilingual parity and accessibility by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, 44px targets, semantic structure, and first-class RTL.
